Our Journey

Redefining the Indian Salon Experience

Founded in 2014 in Defence Colony, New Delhi, GlowCut Salon was built on a simple promise: delivering international luxury hair styling and organic skin treatments with complete price transparency and zero queue waiting headaches.

We realized that customers hated waiting in crowded salon waiting rooms without knowing when their turn would arrive. That's why we engineered our proprietary real-time queue tracking system and AI hairstyle consultation technology.

Today, our team of 15+ certified stylists serves over 2,500 monthly clients while maintaining an impressive 4.9-star rating.
